Apple’s Messages app keeps getting better; here are my favourite updates  

Apple has rolled out a series of tweaks and new features to the Messages app that comes pre‑installed on every iPhone. The changes include a handful of small but handy tools that make chatting a little smoother, plus a few behind‑the‑scenes improvements that keep the app reliable.  

**What this means for you**  
- You’ll notice new ways to organise conversations, such as pinning important chats to the top of the list.  
- Sending photos, voice notes or quick replies is now a touch faster, which can be useful when you’re juggling a grocery run or a work call.  
- The updates arrive automatically with the next iOS refresh, so there’s no extra step required on your part.  

The pros and cons of using your phone as a mobile hotspot  

Engadget has laid out the upside and the downside of turning your smartphone into a Wi‑Fi hotspot for other devices. The feature is handy when you’re away from home Wi‑Fi, but it comes with trade‑offs that most users don’t think about.  

**What this means for you**  
- **Convenient internet access** – You can connect a laptop, tablet or even a smart TV to the internet using your phone’s data plan, perfect for a quick work email on the road.  
- **Battery drain** – The hotspot function can chew through your phone’s battery quickly, so keep a charger handy if you plan to use it for more than a short spell.  
- **Data usage** – Anything streamed or downloaded on the connected device counts against your mobile data allowance; keep an eye on your plan’s limits to avoid unexpected overage charges.  
- **Security tip** – Set a strong, unique password for the hotspot (avoid the default “12345678”) and turn the feature off when you’re done to stop strangers from hopping onto your connection.  

The oldest gadget in your house could be your home network’s biggest security hole  

A new Engadget piece warns that clinging to old routers, modems or network‑enabled appliances can leave your entire home Wi‑Fi vulnerable. Out‑of‑date hardware often stops receiving security patches, meaning known flaws remain exploitable by cyber‑criminals.  

**What this means for you**  
- **Check the age of your router** – If it’s more than five years old, it’s worth looking at a replacement that receives regular firmware updates.  
- **Update firmware** – Even newer devices need the latest software; log into your router’s admin page and apply any available updates.  
- **Change default passwords** – Many older devices still run on the factory‑set “admin/admin” login – replace this with a strong, unique password.  
- **Consider a modest upgrade** – Modern routers often include built‑in security features like automatic malware blocking, which can give you peace of mind without a big expense.  

Apple’s new study looks to improve the effectiveness of AI health tools  

Apple announced a research effort called the Apple Health AI Usage Study, partnering with the clinical AI platform OpenEvidence. Participants will use a brand‑new iOS app to ask medical‑related questions and then rate the answers for accuracy, detail and relevance. The goal is to understand how people interact with conversational AI that offers health information.  

**What this means for you**  
- **Better AI health advice in the future** – The study’s findings could help Apple fine‑tune the way its AI assistants answer medical queries, making them more reliable when you need quick guidance.  
- **No immediate action required** – The study is voluntary and limited to participants, so everyday users won’t see any changes on their phones today.  
- **Privacy reminder** – If you ever use a health‑focused AI app, read the privacy settings and be comfortable with what information is being shared.  

## Quick takeaway  

- **Messages updates** – New organising tools and faster sharing arrive automatically with the next iOS update.  
- **Phone hotspot** – Handy for on‑the‑go internet but watch battery, data use and set a strong password.  
- **Old network gear** – Replace or update any router older than five years, change default passwords, and keep firmware current.  
- **Apple health AI study** – A research project aimed at making AI health answers more accurate; no change for you yet, but keep an eye on future improvements.
