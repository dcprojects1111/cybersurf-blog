The tech world keeps moving, but you don’t have to be a gadget guru to stay in the loop. Here are the most useful consumer updates that could affect the phone, tablet or computer you already own, or the one you might be thinking about buying next.

## Huawei Mate 90 Air leaks its full specs

Huawei has let slip a detailed spec sheet for the upcoming Mate 90 Air, which is slated to be unveiled on 24 November. The phone is said to feature a 7‑inch 1.5K “quad‑curved” display that can refresh 120 times a second (120 Hz) and reach a peak brightness of 4 000 nits – bright enough for sunny outdoor use. Under the hood sits the Kirin 9020 chip, a 6 500 mAh battery that supports 66 W wired charging, and a triple‑camera array (50 MP main, 8 MP ultra‑wide, 12 MP telephoto) plus a 10.7 MP front shooter. The device is only 6.58 mm thick, weighs 208 g and will be offered in a “Red Maple” colour with several memory configurations.

**What this means for you**  
- If you’re looking for a new Android phone, the Mate 90 Air promises a large, bright screen and fast charging – features that matter for watching videos or video‑calling.  
- Huawei’s devices are not yet sold directly in most Australian stores, so keep an eye on Amazon Australia, JB Hi‑Fi or Harvey Norman for local availability and pricing.  
- Because the phone is still a rumor until the official launch, you may want to wait for confirmed Australian pricing before budgeting.

## Apple Pay adds four new features

Apple announced four fresh capabilities for Apple Pay, the service that lets iPhone users pay in stores, online and inside apps. While the headline does not list each feature, Apple says they are designed to make the checkout experience smoother, and a major expansion of the service is already rolling out.

**What this means for you**  
- If you already use Apple Pay, the updates should make paying with your iPhone or Apple Watch even quicker and more reliable.  
- The expansion means more Australian retailers and transport operators may start accepting Apple Pay, so you might find fewer occasions to pull out cash or a card.  
- No extra cost is attached to the new features, but you’ll need the latest iOS version to benefit – check your iPhone’s Settings → General → Software Update.

## Two cheap accessories for iPhone Duo users

A recent post highlighted two Apple accessories that have dropped to their lowest‑ever prices. The USB‑C Apple Pencil, now $49 (regular $79), and the 40 W Dynamic Power Adapter with 60 W max, now $29 (regular $39), are being offered at steep discounts.

**What this means for you**  
- The USB‑C Pencil lets you draw, take notes or navigate your iPhone Duo with a precise tip – useful for school work, recipes or quick sketches.  
- The 40 W adapter can charge your iPhone Duo faster than the standard charger that comes in the box, which is handy if you’re often on the go.  
- Prices shown are U.S. listings; Australian retailers may list the same discount but could have a different final price. Check with your favourite store or carrier before buying.

## iOS 27.2 brings two upgrades to Apple Photos

Apple’s latest iOS 27.2 update includes two new improvements to the built‑in Photos app. While the headline does not detail the changes, Apple says they enhance how you organise and edit your pictures directly on the device.

**What this means for you**  
- If you already keep your family photos on an iPhone, the updates may make sorting and tweaking images easier without needing a computer.  
- The changes arrive automatically when you update to iOS 27.2, so just go to Settings → General → Software Update if you haven’t already.  
- No extra hardware is required, and the update works on any iPhone that supports iOS 27.

## Quick takeaway

- **Huawei Mate 90 Air**: big, bright screen and fast charging; watch Australian retailers for official launch details.  
- **Apple Pay**: smoother checkout and broader acceptance – update iOS to enjoy it.  
- **iPhone Duo accessories**: discounted Pencil and fast charger; confirm local pricing before purchasing.  
- **iOS 27.2 Photos**: new organising and editing tools; update your iPhone to get them now.
